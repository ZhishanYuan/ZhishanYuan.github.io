**Course:** CS 513-A: Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining  
**Role:** Teaching Assistant  
**Instructor:** Prof. Khashayar Dehnad  
**Semester:** Fall 2026  
**Dates:** September 1, 2026 -- December 22, 2026  
**Institution:** Stevens Institute of Technology

This graduate course introduces fundamental and practical tools, techniques, and algorithms for knowledge discovery and data mining. Topics include machine learning, classification trees, regression trees, artificial neural networks, rule-based systems, fuzzy logic, and case studies drawn from real-world applications.

As a teaching assistant, I support course instruction, student learning, and course-related activities.
